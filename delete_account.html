Delete your Iptv Pal account and data

This page explains how users of Iptv Pal can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Iptv Pal account, send an email to:

bedatapro.solutions@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your Iptv Pal account.

Data that will be deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete your account profile, login information, app preferences, and any data associated with your account.

Data that may be retained

We may retain limited data if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or accounting reasons.

Retention period

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Data retained for legal or security reasons may be kept for up to 90 days unless a longer period is required by law.

In-app deletion

You can also delete your account inside the Iptv Pal app by going to: Settings → Legal & Compliance → Delete account.
